# EcoMada - Company Profile & Assessment Context

## 1. Company Overview
**EcoMada** is a rapidly growing e-commerce and agritech company based in Antananarivo, Madagascar. The company connects local Malagasy producers of organic and local agricultural products with urban consumers and international markets.

*   **Industry:** E-commerce & Agritech
*   **Size:** 45 employees
*   **Mission:** Ensure fast, secure deliveries and payments while supporting local farmers.
*   **Current State:** The company has experienced explosive growth over the last 12 months. Management is currently 100% focused on sales and logistics, leaving cybersecurity as a secondary priority.

## 2. Technical Environment & Scope
The assessment focuses on the following IT infrastructure and business operations:
*   **E-commerce Platform:** Hosted on Shopify (managing sales, customer accounts, and payments).
*   **Database:** Customer and order databases hosted on AWS (Amazon Web Services).
*   **Hardware:** 
    *   15 office laptops.
    *   10 Android tablets used by field sales representatives to take orders directly from producers in remote areas.
*   **Communication:** Heavy reliance on WhatsApp Business for customer service and internal coordination.

## 3. Sensitive Data Handled
EcoMada processes highly sensitive information that must be protected:
*   **Customer PII:** Names, physical addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses.
*   **Financial Data:** Online payment details and transaction histories.
*   **Business Data:** Confidential contracts and pricing agreements with local producers.

## 4. Current Cybersecurity Posture
An initial review revealed that EcoMada lacks formal security governance. 
*   There is no written security policy.
*   The IT team consists of a single internal IT specialist (mostly handling user support) and an external web agency (managing Shopify).
*   Passwords are sometimes shared among employees.
*   There is no established procedure for lost devices or security incidents.

## 5. Assessment Objective
The purpose of this GRC assessment is to evaluate EcoMada's security posture against the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0**. The goal is to identify critical gaps, assess their business impact, and provide a prioritized remediation plan to secure customer data and ensure business continuity.
